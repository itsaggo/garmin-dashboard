Weather location

Used for the weather box and heat-graded paces. Weather comes from Open-Meteo; only this place, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to them.

Personal API key

Steps, sleep, heart rate, HRV, Body Battery, stress and activities. Create or renew the key at Garmin Connect → Personal API.

Garmin Connect

Adds VO2 max, training readiness, training status, recovery time, Garmin's race predictor and a backup source for resting heart rate. Your password is used once and never saved.

AI coach

Each morning your coach reads a summary of your numbers and writes a short insight, and you can ask it questions. Only that summary is sent (no name, email or raw files). Cloud providers bill you directly, about a cent per insight, and the dashboard caps use at 8 rewrites and 30 questions a day.

Customize your dashboard

Drag boxes between the main page, the Details tab and Hidden. Drag a box's bottom-right corner to resize it. On a phone, boxes stack full-width in the same order. Keyboard: arrows resize, Alt+arrows reorder.

Discord

Posts to a Discord channel you choose. In Discord: open the channel's Edit Channel → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook, then Copy Webhook URL and paste it here. Treat the link like a password; it's stored privately on your server. Messages include your scores and coach notes, and never ping anyone.
